**Important Notes & Delivery Advice for Software Engineers:**

*   **Focus on Practicality:** This audience *wants* to know how these tools can make their lives easier, not just the theoretical benefits.  Show, don't just tell. Demos are incredibly valuable (even short ones).
*   **Technical Depth – But Not Too Deep:** Avoid overly complex explanations of AI algorithms. Focus on the *outcome* and how it impacts their workflow.
*   **Be Honest About Limitations:** Acknowledge that these tools aren’t perfect.  Highlighting potential pitfalls builds credibility.
*   **Prompt Engineering is Crucial:** Emphasize this – it's a new skill developers need to learn. Give examples of good and bad prompts.
*   **Security & Privacy:** Address security concerns upfront, as software engineers are often very aware of these issues.
*   **Tailor the Tools:** Research which AI tools are most popular or relevant within your audience’s specific tech stack (e.g., if they primarily use Java, focus on tools with strong Java support).
*   **Encourage Experimentation:**  The best way to learn is by doing. Encourage them to try out these tools themselves.
*   **Keep it Concise:** Software engineers are busy people. Get to the point quickly and avoid unnecessary jargon.
